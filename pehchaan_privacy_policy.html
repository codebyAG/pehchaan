Your privacy matters.

A simple explanation of how Pehchaan handles your business information, photos and marketing content.

1. Overview

Pehchaan helps small and local businesses create professional image-based marketing resources from basic business information, photos and promotional details.

Pehchaan V1 does not automatically publish anything for you. You decide where and how to use the creatives you download or share.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Pehchaan, we may collect:

Business information

  • Business or shop name
  • Business category
  • Business location or address
  • Contact details you choose to include
  • Products, services, prices and offers

Photos and uploaded content

You may upload shop photos, product photos, service photos, logos or other images that you want to use in a creative.

Device and usage information

We may collect limited technical information such as device type, app version, operating system information, crash information and basic usage events needed to operate and improve the service.

3. How we use information

  • Create and personalize marketing creatives.
  • Keep your business profile and saved creatives available.
  • Provide, maintain and improve Pehchaan.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud and unauthorized access.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve app performance.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.
Pehchaan V1 does not use your information to automatically post on Instagram, WhatsApp or other platforms on your behalf.

4. Photos and user content

You remain responsible for the photos, logos, product information, prices, contact details and other content you upload.

Please only upload content that you have permission or legal rights to use. Do not upload another person's private information or content without the necessary permission.

Uploaded content may be processed by Pehchaan's service providers when necessary to provide creative-generation and storage features.

5. When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers when they need it to provide services to Pehchaan.

These providers may support:

  • Cloud hosting and storage
  • Creative-generation processing
  • Authentication and account services
  • Crash reporting and app performance
  • Customer support

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety or security of Pehchaan, users or others.

6. Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Pehchaan, maintain saved content, meet legitimate business needs, resolve disputes, enforce agreements or comply with legal obligations.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or otherwise control certain personal information.

To request deletion of your account or information, contact us below. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

9. Children's privacy

Pehchaan is intended for business owners and general users and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as Pehchaan evolves or legal requirements change. When appropriate, material changes may be communicated through the app or another reasonable channel.
